How this stays cheap to host

  • Nothing is re-hosted. This site serves markup, styles, one script and one data file. No video, audio or player code is stored here or proxied through here.
  • Cards are facades. Every tile you see is drawn locally at zero network cost. The <iframe> does not exist until you press play — so browsing the whole wall costs the platforms nothing and costs this host nothing.
  • One player at a time. Starting a second video tears the first iframe out of the page, which is what actually ends the stream. A long session never stacks up live players.
  • Poster art is opt-in. Thumbnails come from platform CDNs, so they are a third-party request too. The toggle is off by default and remembered per browser.
  • When you do press play, the bytes travel from the platform's CDN to your browser directly. They never touch this server's bandwidth bill.
