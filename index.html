How To Play

  • Click a tile on the Dig Grid to dig it up.
  • Each tile hides dirt, rock, or an oil well — and a reward from 0–89.
  • Your total climbs as you reveal more tiles.
  • Use Show All to peek every remaining reward.
  • Use Reset Grid to dig a fresh grid.
