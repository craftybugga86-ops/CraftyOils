How To Play

  • Click a tile on the Dig Grid to dig it up.
  • 🟫 Dirt pays 1–9, 🪨 rock pays 3–27, 🛢️ an oil well pays 10–90 — oil is worth the most, but turns up rarest (a 5:3:1 dirt:rock:oil spawn ratio).
  • Your total climbs as you reveal more tiles.
  • Use Show All to dig up every remaining tile at once.
  • Use Reset Grid to dig a fresh grid.
