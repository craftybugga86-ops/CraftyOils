How To Play

  • Player One, Two, and Three each get one turn of 10 picks, in order — click a tile to dig it up.
  • 🟫 Dirt pays 1–9, 🪨 rock pays 3–27, 🛢️ an oil well pays 10–90 — oil is worth the most, but turns up rarest (a 5:3:1 dirt:rock:oil spawn ratio).
  • As soon as a player uses all 10 picks, it automatically passes to the next player — no need to hand over control yourself.
  • Use Show All to dig up every remaining pick at once, or Reset Grid to reshuffle the current board.
  • Once Player Three's turn ends, the game is over and shows final results, ranked highest to lowest.
