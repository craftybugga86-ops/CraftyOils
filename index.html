How To Play

Three games, one shared stash — what you dig feeds what you craft, and what you craft feeds what you build.

⛏️ Crafty Oils

  • Pick Best of 1, 3, or 5 games before you start (or anytime, to start a new match).
  • In each game, Player One, Two, and Three each get one turn of 10 picks, in order — every tile starts as a "?", so you're digging blind until you click it.
  • 🟫 Dirt pays 1–9, 🪨 rock pays 3–27, 🛢️ an oil well pays 10–90 — oil pays most and turns up most often, while dirt is the scarcest find (a 1:3:5 dirt:rock:oil spawn ratio).
  • As soon as a player uses all 10 picks, it automatically passes to the next player — no need to hand over control yourself.
  • After the last game, scores are tallied across every game played and the match ends, showing final results ranked highest to lowest.

🔨 Crafty Crafting

  • Spend the resources you dug on goods: 🧱 bricks, ⚙️ gears, 🛢️ barrels, 🪟 windows and 🔩 steel beams.
  • Crafting consumes resources for good, so a dug tile can only ever be spent once.

🏘️ Crafty Housing

  • Spend crafted goods on buildings, from a 🏕️ tent up to a 🏰 estate — each one is worth prestige.
  • Buildings stand permanently, and your estate's prestige shows on your Collection card.
